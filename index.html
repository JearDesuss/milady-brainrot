      A place to be a little strange

      Milady Brainrot is a small dress-up collection. Seven Italian brainrot characters were redrawn as Milady-style dolls, with big eyes and lace, and given a drawer of things to wear: two bows, a butterfly clip, a tiara, a pixel halo, a cheek sticker, a sparkle aura and one pair of wings.

      Everything on this page is built from the same layered files, so the look you compose here is the whole work. There is nothing to buy and nothing to join. The point is the picture, and you may keep it.

      The characters are fan reinterpretations of existing internet concepts, not official assets of anyone.
